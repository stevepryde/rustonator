Privacy Policy


Last updated: February 2026

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide: When you play Detonator, you choose a display name. This name is visible to other players and is stored alongside your score on our leaderboard. We do not require account registration or collect email addresses to play.

Information collected automatically: When you connect to our servers, we may collect your IP address, browser type, and connection timestamps. This information is used solely for server operation, security, and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
• Operate and maintain the game
• Display your chosen name and score on leaderboards
• Monitor and prevent abuse or cheating
• Improve game performance and stability

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.

Our website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect basic analytics data (page views, country of origin) in accordance with their privacy policy.

4. Cookies

Detonator does not set any first-party cookies. Third-party services such as Cloudflare may use cookies for security and performance purposes.

5. Data Retention

Leaderboard scores and display names are retained indefinitely. Server logs (including IP addresses) are retained for a limited period for operational purposes and then deleted.

6. Children's Privacy

Detonator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only user-provided data is a display name, which does not need to identify the player in any way.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your leaderboard data by contacting us at the email address below. As we do not require accounts, we do not store data that can be tied to a specific individual beyond what is described above.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please email support@inspiredjourneys.dev.
